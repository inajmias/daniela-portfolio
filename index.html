O2024 –

2024 – present · sponsored by MD Anderson Cancer Center

ORCADEOral Cancer Diagnostic Evaluator — a brush that has to collect a genome without cutting anyone

  • Bioengineering
  • Product design
  • Clinical need
  • Entrepreneurship

Finding oral cancer early currently involves a scalpel, so people put it off. ORCADE is a brush instead — a geometry problem: bristle stiffness, density, how hard you can press before it stops being non-invasive. Printed, tested, reprinted until the yield doubled. Validated from cell culture to human clinical protocol; provisional patent filed.

  • 550 ngDNA per sample
  • 1.92Purity
  • 2×Yield vs. current tools

R2022

2022 · re:3D, Houston · NASA-contractor manufacturing

re:storeHumidity, and why the printed parts kept snapping

  • Sensors
  • Embedded control
  • Manufacturing
  • Houston weather

The printers were turning out brittle parts and everyone was looking at the machines. It was the air — Houston humidity soaks into filament on the shelf. I built the room a nervous system: sensing, automated dehumidification, a touchscreen, Arduino underneath. Filament humidity went from ~50% to under 30%, and stayed there.

  • 50% → <30%Filament humidity
  • Closed loopSense and act, no operator

H2026 –

2026 – present · Founder & CEO

HydroTwinWhat if the plants are a better sensor than the sensors?

  • Machine learning
  • Plant biology
  • IoT infrastructure
  • Agriculture

Growers trust their probes right up until one drifts for three weeks and takes a crop cycle with it. HydroTwin flips the witness: plants respond to the actual environment, so when biology and sensor disagree, the sensor is the thing under suspicion. Random Forest + Isolation Forest, residual logic in between, a dashboard on top.

A2024–25

2024 – 2025 · began in Bologna, Italy

AcqualizerFrom Italy to industry — rethinking liquid mixing through simulation of mechanically assembled systems

  • Fluid dynamics
  • Mechanical design
  • Process equipment
  • Simulation

Bologna's automated food machinery is beautiful and slightly deceptive: the mechanism is the visible part, but what the fluid does inside decides whether it works. Acqualizer models that — an industrial mixer in SolidWorks, flow simulation through it, the same question extended to bioreactors, formulation, and chemical processing.

Counting a forestSpatial foundations for forest climate action in the Peruvian Amazon

S2018–19

2018 – 2019 · Costa Rica · advised by OVSICORI

SALVASFinding people under a collapsed building, automatically

  • Electronics
  • Disaster response
  • Seismology
  • Social venture

Costa Rica sits on a subduction zone. SALVAS is an automated localization system for the first hours after a collapse — built in high school, advised by OVSICORI, the national seismological institute, around one constraint: disaster equipment has to stay simple, deployable, resilient. It won nationally, then internationally in Mexico City.

I move between fields on purpose.
